# Sprint 5 — Usability Testing Script

*Hand this page to each tester (or read it aloud while sharing your screen). Don't help
them unless they're completely stuck for more than a minute — where they hesitate or
get confused IS the data. Takes about 20–30 minutes per person. Aim for 2–3 testers.*

## Before you start

- Use a **fresh account** for each tester (have them register their own, or create one
  per tester ahead of time) — not the demo account, and not your own — so you're
  watching a genuinely new user, not someone who already knows where everything is.
- Just watch and take notes. Don't explain how to do something unless they ask twice,
  or a full minute has passed with no progress.

## The tasks

Give the tester these instructions one at a time, in order, without extra hints:

1. "Create an account and log in."
2. "Create a new research project about any topic you're curious about."
3. "Search for a paper related to that topic, and save one to your project."
4. "Open the paper you just saved and generate an AI summary of it."
5. "Add a short note to that paper."
6. "Save one more paper to the same project, then open the Literature Matrix and
   extract its fields with AI."
7. "Generate a Paper Synthesis across the two papers you've saved."
8. "Open Ask the Literature and ask a question about your saved papers."
9. "Switch the app to dark mode, then switch it back."
10. "Log out."

## Three questions to ask at the end

1. "Was there any point where you weren't sure what to click next?"
2. "Did anything take longer than you expected?"
3. "Is there anything you'd change about how this works?"

## Turning this into action

After each session, pull out anything that happened on **more than one tester's**
sheet — a single person's confusion might just be them, but the same hesitation from
two or three different people is a real usability issue. Add those to Sprint 5's bug
table in `Sprint5_Plan.md` (item 7).
